## Main text

Hotel group branding is a long game. A group grows, hotels are added and renovated, seasons come and go, and the brand has to stay recognisable through all of it. Mediterranean Hotels, with hotels on the Olympus Riviera in Pieria and a growing presence in Mykonos, has trusted us with that task since 1997.

## A partnership since 1997

Almost three decades of work together is the foundation of this project. We have grown alongside the group: from its early years to today's family of hotels, designing identities, stationery, printed material, websites and campaigns as each new chapter began.

In hotel group branding, that continuity matters. Every new hotel, renovation or season starts from the same visual language, so guests who return year after year recognise the group immediately, and new guests see one confident brand rather than a collection of separate businesses.

## A monogram for the whole family

At the centre of the identity is the M monogram. A bold, elegant M is framed by baroque scrolls, finished with a flourish beneath and crowned with a small trident and a heart. The trident speaks of the sea and of Poseidon, at home on any Greek coast; the heart speaks of hospitality, the warmth guests feel when they arrive.

Beneath the monogram, MEDITERRANEAN HOTELS is set in clean, open capitals, followed by the places the group calls home: Olympus Riviera, Mykonos, Greece. The contrast between the ornate symbol and the calm lettering gives the brand its character, classic and welcoming without feeling heavy.

## Hotel group branding that lets each hotel shine

Good hotel group branding works like a family name. The monogram and the group name tie everything together, while each hotel keeps its own personality: the Mediterranean Village Hotel & Spa, the adults-only Mediterranean Princess and the Mediterranean Resort on the Olympus Riviera each speak to different guests, from families to couples looking for calm.

We designed the system so that hotel names can sit beside the group mark in the same typography, with their own accents where needed. Guests always know which hotel they are staying in, and which family it belongs to.

## From the Olympus Riviera to Mykonos

The group's hotels sit in two very different settings. In Pieria, the beaches of Paralia Katerinis look up at Mount Olympus, with green slopes, long sandy shores and family holidays. In Mykonos, the island's light and its international guests call for a more polished, cosmopolitan tone.

The hotel group branding was built to work in both. Its calm colours, generous spacing and classic monogram feel equally right in a family resort, an adults-only retreat and an island setting.

## Stationery, print and signage

Over the years, as part of the hotel group branding, we have designed the printed world of the group: stationery and business cards, brochures and guest information, and signage from reception to the room door. Each piece follows the same rules for type, spacing and the use of the monogram, so even the smallest card feels part of the whole.

The monogram translates beautifully to physical materials. Etched on glass at reception, printed in one colour on paper or embroidered on linen, it keeps its detail and its elegance.

## Websites and art direction

As booking moved online, the brand moved with it. We designed websites for the group and directed photography and campaigns that show the hotels, the sea and the mountain in a consistent, inviting light. Online and in print, the hotel group branding tells the same story.

## Consistency across decades

The hardest part of hotel group branding is not creating a logo but caring for it for years. We keep the identity consistent while letting it evolve gently with each renovation, each new property and each change in how guests travel and book. The result is a brand that feels established and trusted, and still up to date.

## Why this hotel group branding works

- **It holds a family together.** One monogram and one typographic system connect every hotel in the group.
- **It lets each hotel be itself.** Individual hotel names and accents sit comfortably within the family look.
- **It has stood the test of time.** Since 1997 the same core identity has carried the group through growth and change.

For almost thirty years, Mediterranean Hotels and VASSILISDESIGN have built this brand together, and the work continues with every new season.
