## Main text

A legal network website has a different job from a law firm's site. It does not only speak to clients; it has to convince other law firms to join. For LEInternational, a network of independent law firms founded in 1991, with 800 lawyers and 54 offices in 31 countries, we designed a website with one clear purpose: to attract quality firms from around the world and invite them to connect.

## One purpose: inviting firms to join

LEI is growing and actively looking for dynamic, quality-driven firms. So every page of the legal network website leads in the same direction. A bright orange Join Us button stays visible from the first screen, and a dedicated page explains, in plain language, what a firm gains: cross-border referrals from member firms in more than 40 jurisdictions, knowledge sharing in specialist teams, international credibility, and access to annual and regional meetings, webinars and workshops.

The same page states what LEI looks for in a member: mid-sized firms with strong domestic practices and international capabilities, a commitment to responsiveness, integrity and collaboration, and partner-level involvement. A firm can see in a few minutes whether it is a good fit, and take the next step.

## Eight oars, one rhythm

The home page opens with a single, memorable image: a rowing crew seen from above, gliding across deep blue water. Each oar is a different colour, yet all of them move in the same rhythm. It is exactly what LEI is: independent firms with their own identity, working together toward the same goal.

Over the image, the network's promise is set in an elegant serif: Local expertise, global network. Two short links lead to the international firm network and the specialist teams, and the Join Us button invites the visitor aboard.

## Legal network website design built on trust

Lawyers choose partners carefully, so the design had to feel credible from the first second. Deep teal gives the legal network website a calm, established tone; warm orange is used sparingly, only for the actions that matter. Classic serif headings bring authority, while clean body text keeps long pages easy to read.

Trust is also built with facts and faces. A band of numbers shows the network's scale at a glance: 800 lawyers, 31 countries, 54 law offices and a foundation year of 1991. The CEO, Pablo Bonfill, introduces LEI in his own words: a community built on trust. Vision, mission and values sit beside him, short and clear.

## A network you can see

A network is only as strong as its members, so the website puts them on show. Visitors can explore the international firm network, the specialist teams and the board, and see the firms behind each jurisdiction. Sponsors have their own showcase, from bronze to platinum, including FBFK Law, whose website we also designed (see our [FBFK Law project](https://vassilisdesign.gr/works/fbfk-law-firm-website-design/)).

Member testimonials complete the picture. Partners from firms around the world explain, in their own words, what the network has meant for their international practice and for the relationships they have built.

## Events that bring people together

LEI is a living community, and its events are a big part of why firms join. The website gives the Annual Conference its own section, with upcoming meetings such as Shanghai in 2026 and Portugal in 2027, together with online annual meetings, specialist group conferences, webinars and legal news from members across jurisdictions. On a legal network website, regular, well-presented updates show prospective members an active, engaged network.

## For members, too

Beyond attracting new firms, the legal network website serves the people already inside. A Members Area and online payments keep membership practical, while news and event pages make it easy for members to share their work with the whole network.

## Designed for every screen

Partners browse a legal network website on a phone between meetings and on a large screen at the office. Every section, from the rowing crew to the member reviews, adapts to desktop, laptop, tablet and phone without losing its clarity or its calls to action.

## Why this legal network website works

- **It has one clear goal.** Every page leads prospective firms toward Join Us.
- **It proves the value.** Numbers, testimonials, events and sponsors show a real, active network.
- **It feels like a community.** The rowing crew, the CEO's welcome and the members' voices make trust visible.

The result is a legal network website that works as LEI's best recruiter, open to firms everywhere. Visit it at [lei-network.com](https://lei-network.com).
