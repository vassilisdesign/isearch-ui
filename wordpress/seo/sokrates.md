## Main text

Furniture catalogue design starts long before the layout. A chair is bought for how it looks, how it feels and how it fits a room, so the photographs have to do most of the selling. For Sokrates chairs&more, a Thessaloniki company with a long history in making chairs and tables for homes and professional spaces, we have created the product photography, the catalogues and the website since 2021.

## Photography that makes furniture feel light

Every project for Sokrates begins with the photo shoot. We treat each piece like a portrait: soft daylight, calm walls and floors, and only a few props, usually green leaves, so the eye goes straight to the shape, the wood and the fabric. A curved armchair in raspberry velvet stands beside a white panelled door; a grey fabric chair sits against a pale blue wall under palm leaves.

Sometimes one image needs to stop the reader. On one catalogue cover a pink balloon lifts a teal tufted chair into the air. It is a simple idea with a clear message: Sokrates furniture is solid and well made, but it never feels heavy. In furniture catalogue design, one image like this can make a whole collection memorable.

## Furniture catalogue design for home and professional spaces

Sokrates serves two kinds of customers: families furnishing a home, and professionals furnishing the spaces where they welcome their own customers. The furniture catalogue design speaks to both. Each cover carries the line Οικιακός & Επαγγελματικός Εξοπλισμός, home and professional equipment, and each catalogue has its own mood, from a bright white and blush cover to a calm grey one and the playful balloon.

Inside, the layouts stay simple. Large photographs fill the pages, and each model gets its name and dimensions in small, clear type, such as the Bronx chair at 79x61x58cm or the Danae at 85x51x56cm. A buyer can compare models quickly, and a home customer can picture the chair in a real room.

## A clear system, page after page

Good furniture catalogue design is a system, not a series of single pages. A consistent grid, a restrained palette and the orange accent of the chairs&more logo hold every catalogue together, so new collections can be added season after season without losing the brand's calm, confident look.

## A website built like a showroom

The website continues the furniture catalogue design on screen. Its menu is short: Home, Products, Catalogue, Company and Contact. Products are grouped into clear categories such as chairs, and every product page shows the model's name, its product code and colour swatches, ready for a customer's enquiry or a professional's order. Visitors can also browse the catalogue online.

The same photography runs through the whole site, so customers meet the same Sokrates in print and online. Every page works on desktop, tablet and phone, for a buyer on a showroom visit as much as for a family choosing chairs on the sofa.

## Why this furniture catalogue design works

- **The photographs sell.** Calm, styled images show each chair's shape, materials and character.
- **One brand everywhere.** Catalogues and website share the same photography, type and colours.
- **It speaks to two audiences.** Homes and professional spaces find what they need quickly.

Since 2021 every new collection has given us a reason to return to the studio, and to show once more how good a well-made chair can look.
