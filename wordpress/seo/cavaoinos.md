## Main text

Wine importer branding has a special task: to make a whole country's wines feel desirable in a new market. For Cava Oinos, the New York importer of premium Greek wines, that means bringing the history, landscapes and craftsmanship of Greece to sommeliers, restaurants, retailers and collectors across the United States. We have been shaping how Cava Oinos speaks since 2015.

## Since 2015: a partnership across the Atlantic

Over more than ten years we have designed almost everything Cava Oinos puts in front of its customers: the identity, the website, brochures and flyers, promotional material, invitations to tastings and events, and much more. As the importer's portfolio found its place in many of New York City's most prestigious establishments and began to grow in new markets such as Florida, the brand grew with it.

In wine importer branding, working with one studio for so long gives a rare consistency. Every new winery, campaign or event starts from the same visual language, so buyers recognise Cava Oinos at a glance, whether they see a brochure on a restaurant table or the website on their phone.

## Wine importer branding with Greek soul and New York polish

The identity balances two worlds. Deep wine red, warm neutral tones and refined typography speak of elegance and confidence, the qualities a New York wine list expects. Imagery full of warmth, people and shared moments speaks of Greece, where wine has been part of daily life for thousands of years.

Good wine importer branding never competes with the wines. It frames them. Our layouts give each label, region and grape variety the space to be noticed, so the story of the wine always comes first.

## Presenting Greece's finest wineries

Cava Oinos represents a portfolio of respected Greek producers, and much of our work introduces them to the American market. Among them are Domaine Porto Carras in Halkidiki, the largest organic vineyard in Greece; Dougos in Rapsani on Mount Olympus, where myth says the gods made their wine; Wine Art Estate at Mikrochori, below Mount Pangaion; Santorini Kalliston with its PDO wines from the volcanic island; and Lantides, a family winery founded in 1993. Moschopolis, Pieria Eratini, Samartzis, Monsieur Nicolas and OENO Π complete the collection.

Each winery has its own character, and our wine importer branding respects it. The materials we create for the US market keep every producer recognisable while presenting the whole portfolio with one clear, premium Cava Oinos look.

## Brochures and flyers for the US market

Printed material is still where many wine sales begin. We design brochures and flyers that guide sommeliers, buyers and retailers through the portfolio: the regions, the wineries, the wines and their varieties, with tasting notes and pairing ideas written for American readers. Clear structure and beautiful photography make Greek grapes such as Assyrtiko, Xinomavro and Agiorgitiko approachable for buyers meeting them for the first time.

This is where wine importer branding works hardest: a well-designed trade brochure helps a wine find its way onto a list.

## Invitations, tastings and promotional material

Wine is best discovered in a glass. We design invitations and materials for Cava Oinos tastings and events, together with promotional pieces for campaigns and points of sale. Each one carries the same warm, refined look, so an invitation already feels like the start of the evening.

The wine importer branding also extends to Cava Oinos Moments, the importer's hosted tastings and wine paths in Greece, which share its passion for Greek wine with friends and partners.

## A website that tells the story of Greek wine

We designed and built [cavaoinos.com](https://cavaoinos.com/), the digital heart of the wine importer branding, as an invitation to explore. Visitors move through the regions, from Olympus to Santorini, meet the wineries, browse the wines by type and discover native grape varieties. A page on the importer's vision presents its rare older vintages, such as Château Porto Carras 1990 and Porfyrogenitos 1993 by Émile Peynaud, for collectors looking for wines of extraordinary lineage.

A line from Euripides greets visitors on the home page: where there is no wine, there is no love. It sums up the tone of the whole site, passionate, cultured and welcoming.

## Why this wine importer branding works

- **It sells a country, not just a label.** History, regions and grapes are presented in a way American buyers can enjoy and understand.
- **It respects every winery.** Each producer keeps its own character inside one premium portfolio.
- **It has lasted more than ten years.** Since 2015 every brochure, invitation and web page has spoken with the same confident voice.

For more than a decade Cava Oinos has trusted us to tell the story of Greek wine in New York, and every new vintage gives us a new chapter to design.
