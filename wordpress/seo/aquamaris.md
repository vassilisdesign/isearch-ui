## Main text

Tour operator branding has to sell something nobody can hold: a week by the sea, months before it happens. For Aquamaris, a Polish tour operator offering holidays in Greece and Bulgaria, we created the logo and identity, directed the photography and designed the yearly holiday catalogues, ads and printed material that brought Polish travellers to the Aegean and the Black Sea.

## A seahorse that travels

The name already speaks of the sea, so the logo stays close to it. A slender seahorse rises above the lowercase wordmark aquamaris, drawn in a deep, calm blue. The seahorse is a small traveller that moves with the current, a gentle symbol for a company that carries people to the water. Below it, a line sums up the promise: Beyond destinations. A holiday is more than a place on the map; it is the light, the water and the time you finally have for yourself.

## Tour operator branding that sells a feeling

People rarely choose a holiday by reading a price table first. They choose a feeling, then look for the hotel and the date that fit it. Our tour operator branding for Aquamaris follows that order. Turquoise, white and sunlight lead every piece; the details follow, clear and easy to compare.

The summer catalogue for 2011 shows the idea at its best. The cover opens on a swimmer floating in crystal-clear turquoise water, seen from above, with the destinations along the bottom: Crete, Corfu, Rhodes, Thassos, the Olympic Riviera and Sunny Beach in Bulgaria. No slogan is needed. The reader is already in the water.

## Catalogues, season after season

For several seasons we designed the Aquamaris holiday catalogues, the core sales tool of any tour operator. Each one brings many hotels and resorts together in one clear system. Every hotel page follows the same structure: large photographs, a short introduction, and clear sections for the location, meals, sports and entertainment, and facilities for children. Price tables list each departure date for 7 and 14 day stays, so families can plan and compare in minutes.

Consistency is what makes tour operator branding work in print. Whether a reader opens the catalogue at Crete or at the Bulgarian coast, the layout, the colours and the tone stay the same, and Aquamaris stays recognisable on every page.

## Photography that makes you want to go

Holiday photography decides whether a page is turned or remembered. We directed the photography and chose images that show what travellers dream of: blue pools, golden beaches, palm trees, generous breakfasts and children playing in the water. Bright, natural colour and wide views of the sea give every destination the same warm, inviting light.

## Ads and printed material

Beyond the catalogues, we designed ads and other printed pieces for the Polish market. Each one extends the tour operator branding and carries the seahorse, the turquoise palette and the promise Beyond destinations, so every touchpoint feels like part of one journey.

## Why this tour operator branding works

- **It sells the feeling first.** Clear water and sunlight lead; prices and details follow.
- **It is easy to compare.** One structure for every hotel and clear price tables for every date.
- **It is one brand everywhere.** Logo, catalogues, photography and ads share one look, season after season.

For Aquamaris, we turned holidays in Greece and Bulgaria into a brand Polish travellers could trust and look forward to.
