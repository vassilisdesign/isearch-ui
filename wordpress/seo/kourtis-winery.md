## Main text

Some projects are a job. This one is a friendship. We have known Apostolos Kourtis since 1992 and have worked with Kourtis Winery since 2000, two years before its first harvest. Winery branding over such a long time is not a single logo or a single label: it is a conversation, season after season, about how a small family estate in Pieria should look, speak and grow.

## From tobacco fields to award-winning wine

The story of the estate is the heart of its winery branding. For generations the Kourtis family grew mainly tobacco in Rachi, a village of about 500 people built on a ridge between Mount Olympus and the Pierian Mountains. The family always kept a small vineyard for its own wine and tsipouro. At the end of the 1990s, as tobacco reached a dead end, they decided to plant vines professionally.

The first vineyard was planted in 1997, the first harvest came in 2002 and, in 2006, a modern winery opened in the old family premises. Today six hectares of organic vineyards, certified since 2006, grow Greek and international varieties at 300 metres, from Assyrtiko, Xinomavro and Agiorgitiko to Syrah and Cabernet Sauvignon, and since 2015 the rare Greek Vidiano. We have been part of that journey from the beginning, and our winery branding has grown with it.

## Creative obsession, incomparable nature

The estate's motto, creative obsession, incomparable nature, sums up our winery branding approach too. If Olympus is the home of the gods, Pieria is the home of the Muses, and the brand draws on both: on one side the mountains, the vineyards and the light; on the other, a family that does everything with its own hands, from planting the vines to serving meze in the tasting room.

The identity stays calm and classic, with a refined serif wordmark and quiet colours, so the landscape and the wines can take the lead. This is the balance we look for in all winery branding: confident enough to be recognised, modest enough to let the place speak.

## Labels, and the advice behind them

Over the years we have designed labels and advised the family on every new wine and every change in the range: the Oinovion signature wines, white and red; Ino, a Trebbiano aged in new oak; Nychta, made mostly from Vidiano; Eos, a Xinomavro and Syrah rosé; Xinomavro; and Oneiros, the estate's award-winning blend. Each wine has its own name, often from Greek mythology, and its own character, while the family look stays clear on the shelf.

The Aeraki range shows our winery branding at its simplest. Aeraki means little breeze in Greek. Each label carries one translucent flower, pale yellow for the whites, pink for the rosé and a red poppy for the red, with the name in light, widely spaced capitals on cream paper. The petals look as if they are lifted by the wind, a nod to the cool night winds that sweep the vineyards of Rachi in summer. On the semi-sweet white, a butterfly lands beside the flower.

## A website that opens on Olympus

The website welcomes visitors with the view the family sees every day: snow-capped Olympus above rows of vines, and the motto across the sky. From there, visitors explore the wines one by one, with tasting notes and food pairings, the story of the winery, the vineyard and its climate, photos and the distinctions the wines have won.

Wine tourism is a big part of the estate's life, so a Book a Tour invitation sits on the home page, next to a photo of a table set with wine and meze. Visitors learn about the tasting room, the family's own cheese, meat and bread, and the ramps that make every space accessible. Good winery branding online should make people want to visit, not just to buy.

## Proud of the medals

Kourtis keeps production small and farming organic, and the results show. Oneiros has won a gold medal at the Berliner Wine Trophy, a Decanter World Wine Awards Commended, gold at the Thessaloniki International Wine Competition and silver at the Asia Wine Trophy. Oinovion red, Ino, Nychta and Xinomavro have won medals too. We give these distinctions their own page and place them next to each wine, so a small estate can show its quality with confidence: winery branding that is backed by results.

## Posters, brochures and flyers

Beyond labels and the web, we design the printed pieces that carry the estate to fairs, wine shops, restaurants and events: posters, brochures, flyers and more. Each extends the winery branding with the same identity and the same respect for the landscape, so wherever people meet Kourtis Winery, they meet one brand.

## Why this winery branding works

- **It tells a true story.** From tobacco fields to medals, the brand is built on the family's own journey.
- **It lets the place lead.** Olympus, Pieria and the vineyards are always in the picture.
- **It has grown together for more than 25 years.** One studio, one family, one consistent voice since 2000.

After more than two decades of friendship and work, every new vintage at Kourtis still feels like a new beginning, for the wine and for the design. Visit the estate at [kourtiswinery.com](https://kourtiswinery.com/).
