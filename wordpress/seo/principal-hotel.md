## Main text

Hotel website design is where most holidays begin today. Long before guests feel the sea breeze, they scroll through photos, compare rooms and decide whether a hotel feels right. For Hotel Principal, a three-star hotel in Paralia Katerini, just 250 metres from the Thermaic Gulf and below Mount Olympus, we created the whole guest journey on paper and on screen: the logo and identity, the website, brochures, flyers and menus.

## A crest made for summer

The identity starts with the name. Principal suggests something first and finest, so the logo borrows the language of a classic grand hotel: a refined serif wordmark beneath an ornamental crest crowned with three stars. On its own that could feel formal. So we added a line in flowing script, new leisure hotel, which turns the crest into an invitation to relax.

Colour completes the mood. Aegean blue and turquoise speak of the pool and the gulf; warm bronze adds a touch of elegance. Together they give Principal a look that is polished but never stiff, exactly right for a summer hotel by the sea.

## Hotel website design that feels like arriving

The website opens on a wide, sunny view of the hotel: the blue building, the palm trees, the pool and the shaded pool bar. In a few seconds the visitor knows where they are and what the stay will feel like. This is the first rule of good hotel website design: show the experience before you describe it.

The menu stays short and clear: Accommodation, Facilities, Location, Gallery, Contact and, always visible, Book Online. The rooms page presents every option, from standard and superior double rooms to family suites and a deluxe junior suite with spa bath. Facilities, from the seasonal pool and pool bar to the garden and BBQ, are listed simply, so families and couples find what matters to them quickly.

## The Olympus Riviera, one click away

A hotel sells its surroundings as much as its rooms. The Location section turns Principal into a base for exploring the Olympus Riviera: the mythical mountain and its trails, the archaeological site of Dion, traditional stone villages such as Palios Panteleimonas, and Thessaloniki only 40 minutes away. For guests planning more than a beach holiday, this part of the hotel website design adds real value.

Inside the stay, a Hotel Guide page takes over from the printed folder: check-in times, reception hours and services, all on the guest's phone. The site is available in English and Greek, and the hotel website design adapts to desktop, tablet and phone.

## Brochures and flyers: leisure and pleasure on paper

Printed material still matters in a resort town, at travel fairs, at the reception desk and in the hands of tour operators. We designed a family of brochures that share one look: Aegean blue covers, rounded photo frames and the Principal crest. A pocket brochure and a folded brochure carry the lines leisure & pleasure and leisure & style over images of the pool at sunset, the bar and the rooms. A long accordion brochure opens to present the hotel, its facilities and its rooms in Greek and English.

## A menu to match

The identity reaches the table too. The bilingual food menu sets salads, pasta, grill dishes, pizza and fresh fish on clean white pages, with ingredients flying through the air around each dish. Light, fresh and colourful, it brings the same energy as the pool bar it serves.

## Why this hotel website design works

- **It sells the experience first.** Big, sunny images show the stay before any text.
- **It makes booking easy.** Short menus, clear rooms and Book Online always in view.
- **It is one brand everywhere.** Logo, website, brochures and menus share one crest and one palette.

The result is a hotel website design and identity that welcomes guests to Principal long before they reach the pool. Visit the hotel at [principal-paralia.gr](https://principal-paralia.gr).
