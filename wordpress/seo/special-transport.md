## Main text

Transport company website design has to carry weight, literally. ST Special Transport moves loads most companies would never touch: oversized machinery, heavy lifts, military equipment and dangerous goods. Founded in Thessaloniki in 1974 by Nikolaos Stefanidis and led for more than a decade by Dimitrios Stefanidis, the company now works across the Balkans, Europe and Western Asia. Our transport company website design had to feel as strong and as precise as the work itself.

## A road that never ends

The idea came from the road. A wide red curve sweeps through every page, like a lane on a mountain pass, and frames the main image of each section. On the company page it holds a black and white photograph of an early truck, with 1974 on one side and 2026 on the other: more than fifty years in one glance.

The home page sets the tone with two lines: Moving the world forward, and Because impossible for us is just another route. Large white capitals on black, with one word in red, make every headline read like a road sign: short, clear and impossible to miss.

## Transport company website design in black and red

Heavy transport is serious work, so the palette is simple and strong. Deep black gives weight and focus, red adds energy and urgency, and white keeps the text clear. In this transport company website design, colour is used with discipline: red marks the keyword, the active service and the next step, nothing more.

Photography is treated with the same care. Images of trucks, cranes and convoys sit inside the red curve, often in black and white or tinted red, so the whole site feels like one story rather than a collection of pictures.

## Six services, one clear route

ST offers six services, and each one has its own page and its own headline:

- **Special Transport** for oversized and heavy loads.
- **Military Transport**: We are always on mission, with security, confidentiality and the permits and escorts the law requires.
- **ADR Transports** for dangerous goods.
- **Permits and Escort**: We see the road before you do, with authorities across the Balkans, Central Europe and Turkey.
- **Heavy Lift**: We lift what others can't, illustrated by an ant lifting a stone sphere.
- **Route Survey**: Everything calculated, before a single wheel turns.

A row of service buttons repeats on every page, so a logistics manager can jump from heavy lift to permits in one click. Good transport company website design works like good route planning: no dead ends.

## Proof on every page

Clients in this field choose partners they can trust with valuable, sometimes sensitive cargo. The website gives them proof. A Certifications page explains what ISO 9001, ISO 39001 for road traffic safety and ISO 45001 for occupational health and safety mean in practice, and presents the company's membership in the Hellenic association of international freight forwarding and logistics enterprises. An Our Work gallery shows real loads on real roads: every photo tells the company's story.

## Bilingual and ready for the road

ST works with partners across many countries, so the site is fully bilingual, in English and Greek, with a simple switch at the top of every page. The transport company website design adapts to desktop, laptop, tablet and phone, because a project manager often checks a partner from a site office or the cab of a truck.

## Why this transport company website design works

- **It looks as strong as the fleet.** Black, red and bold capitals give the brand weight and confidence.
- **It is easy to navigate.** Six services, one button row, no dead ends.
- **It builds trust.** History since 1974, ISO certifications and real project photos speak for the company.
