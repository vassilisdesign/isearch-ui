## Main text

Wine label design is often the first conversation a winery has with a new customer. On a crowded shelf or a long wine list, the label has a few seconds to say where the wine comes from and why it is worth choosing. For Dougos Winery, a family estate on the north-east slopes of Mount Olympus, we designed the label for Mavrotragano and rebranded the winery's best-known series, the Rapsani wines.

## A family winery at the foot of Olympus

The Dougos family began its journey in Greek wine in 1991. Today the second generation, Thanos Dougos, an agriculturist, and Louiza Dougou, a chemist and oenologist, cultivate nine hectares of organic vineyards at 550 to 700 metres, in Prosilia and Tourtoura. The soil is poor, iron-rich schist; farming is dry and organic, with low yields. The winery stands in the Tempi Valley, with views of Olympus, Kissavos and the Pinios river, and its wines travel to twelve countries.

That story, a mountain, a family and a strict respect for nature, is the starting point of every wine label design we create for Dougos.

## Mavrotragano: the winemaker's reward

Mavrotragano is a rare red grape, and Dougos makes a powerful, elegant wine from it: roses and spice, full body, 18 months in oak and a long life ahead. Critics agree, with 92+ points from Yiannis Karakasis MW for the 2012 vintage and 91 from Robert Parker for the 2011.

For this label we wanted to honour the people behind the wine. Where the white paper ends, the black glass of the bottle takes over and forms a silhouette: a winemaker in a hat, his head slightly bowed. He is tired after a long day in the vineyard, but not so tired that he cannot lift his eyes and proudly look at what he has made. It is the quiet moment when the day's labour is rewarded, and every bottle of Mavrotragano carries it.

The white paper carries fine hand-drawn lines that give it texture and movement, like rows in a vineyard worked by hand. The name, Mavrotragano, and the vintage are set in a refined, classic serif; the Dougos signature with its small purple flower closes the label. The result is a wine label design that is quiet and confident, and tells its story only to those who look a second time.

## The Rapsani series, rebranded

Rapsani is one of the most respected red wine appellations in Greece, and its vineyards are home ground for Dougos. The winery makes three Rapsani wines from the traditional blend of Xinomavro, Krasato and Stavroto: Rapsani PDO, savoury and elegant, with cedar, smoke and red fruit; Rapsani PDO Old Vines, from low-yielding old vines, with herbs, incense and bright red fruit, a Decanter Expert's Choice; and the Single Vineyard Tourtoura, from one of the estate's own high vineyards.

Our task was a complete wine label design refresh for the whole series. All three labels now share one shape: a classic cartouche with softly cut corners, the name Rapsani written in a flowing hand script, and the Dougos signature below. What changes is the colour, and it tells the level at a glance. The classic Rapsani PDO wears a clean white label with the name in red. Rapsani Old Vines steps up to a bold red label around a white panel. The Single Vineyard Tourtoura Old Vines, the top of the range, is dressed in black with a gold panel and gold lettering.

White, red, black and gold: with this wine label design, a customer, a sommelier or an importer can read the range immediately, from the first bottle to the single vineyard, while the shared shape and script keep the Dougos Rapsani recognisable on any shelf or wine list.

## Wine label design for shelves and wine lists

A good wine label design works in many places at once. It must stand out on a shop shelf, look elegant on a restaurant table, photograph well for social media and an export catalogue, and still feel personal when a visitor buys a bottle at the winery. Clean layouts, classic typography and restrained colours give the Dougos labels that versatility, here and in the twelve countries where the wines are sold.

## Why this wine label design works

- **It tells a human story.** The winemaker's silhouette honours the work behind every bottle.
- **It respects the wine.** Calm, refined design suits wines made for time and patience.
- **It organises the range.** The Rapsani series reads as one family with three clear levels.

For a family that has worked its vineyards on Olympus since 1991, we designed labels that show the pride of that work on every bottle.
