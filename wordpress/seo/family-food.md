## Main text

Food packaging design is at its best when it is part of a long story. For Family Food, a Thessaloniki company that has supplied professionals in Greece and abroad since 2008, we have been writing that story since 2009, almost from the very beginning. Labels, packaging, catalogues, recipe books, brand identities and the company website: over the years we have designed nearly everything the company puts in front of a customer.

## Growing together since 2009

When we started working together, Family Food was a young wholesale company with a clear idea: quality products, stable specifications and long-term trust with its partners. Today it runs a whole portfolio of its own brands next to the products it distributes, in cheeses, dairy, vegetable fats and a fast-growing plant-based range.

We have grown with it, and so has our food packaging design. New brands, new products and new markets have each needed their own look, and each one has been designed to sit comfortably within the family. That continuity is the quiet strength of the partnership: the people who designed the first labels still look after the newest ones.

## Food packaging design for the shelf and the kitchen

Family Food works only with professionals, in two very different worlds. In Retail, a pack has a second or two to be noticed on a busy supermarket shelf. In HoReCa, chefs, bakers and pizza makers need to find the right product fast, from 150 g packs to 4 kg blocks and 13 kg tubs.

Our food packaging design answers both. Each pack leads with a clear photograph of the dish the product is for, names the product in large, friendly type and states its use in a short line: ideal for sandwiches, for cooking, for salads. Badges such as 100% pure milk or 30% less fat sit in the same place every time, so information is quick to scan for shoppers and professionals alike.

## FAMILY: the dish comes first

The FAMILY cheese range shows the system at work. A purple band carries the red FAMILY logo with its small green sprout, and above it a photograph shows the cheese where it belongs: melted over baked potatoes, layered in a sandwich, scattered over a salad or grated over pasta. Gouda, edam, cheddar, smoked cheese, flakes and grated kefalotyri each keep the same layout, so the range lines up as one family on the shelf while every product tells its own use: food packaging design as a system, not a one-off.

## ΕβΛΟΓΗΜΕΝΟ νηστίσιμο: a flagship for fasting and plant-based tables

ΕβΛΟΓΗΜΕΝΟ νηστίσιμο is Family Food's flagship brand and one of the strongest names in its category: plant-based, non-dairy alternatives for the fasting days of the Greek Orthodox calendar and for everyone who chooses plant-based food. The range covers classic and cheddar-flavour slices, a grated three-cheese-style mix and a white product in brine, in sizes for both homes and professional kitchens.

We work on ΕβΛΟΓΗΜΕΝΟ's food packaging design and communication. The challenge is a delicate one: the brand has to feel warm, trustworthy and close to tradition, while making it absolutely clear that the product is plant-based and not dairy. Good food packaging design does both at once, so the customer feels confident choosing it for the family table.

## Happy Vegan and Happy Family

As more people eat flexibly, Family Food added Happy Vegan, plant-based products for retail and HoReCa that behave beautifully in cooking. The brand's gouda-flavour slices won two awards at the Vegan Awards 2023, Bronze for Plant-Based Cheese and Silver for Best Gluten Free Product, a proud moment for a new product.

For Happy Family, the everyday range, we created a bold red logo and food packaging design that shows the dish on every pack. You can see that work in our [Happy Family project](https://vassilisdesign.gr/works/happy-family-food-label-design/).

## ΠΕΤΡΑC: tradition on the shelf

Our food packaging design for the ΠΕΤΡΑC range brings Greek cheeses such as graviera, kefalotyri and talagani to the shelf with the warmth of a village cheese shop: coloured wood, a cream banner and traditional lettering. We also designed Ταξίδι Γεύσεων, a recipe book built around Ταλαγάνι Πέτρας, which turns a product into a reason to cook. More in our [Petras cheese project](https://vassilisdesign.gr/works/petras-cheese-label-design/).

## Catalogues, recipe books and print

Behind every launch, next to the food packaging design, there is printed material. We design Family Food's product catalogues for Retail and HoReCa, product presentations for buyers and partners, and recipe books that inspire both chefs and home cooks. Clear grids, consistent photography and one visual language keep hundreds of products easy to browse, page after page.

## A website made for professionals

We also designed and built [familyfood.gr](https://familyfood.gr), the company's home online. Products are organised the way professionals look for them, by Retail and HoReCa and by category, from plant-based to cheeses, dairy and vegetable fats. Brands have their own pages, catalogues can be downloaded in a click, and recipes with ΕβΛΟΓΗΜΕΝΟ νηστίσιμο and Ταλαγάνι Πέτρας keep visitors coming back.

## Why this partnership works

- **It sees the whole picture.** One studio behind the brands, the packaging, the print and the website keeps everything consistent.
- **It sells at a glance.** Food packaging design that shows the dish and states the use helps products move, on the shelf and in the kitchen.
- **It lasts.** Since 2009 the same care has gone into every label, from the first FAMILY pack to the newest plant-based launch.

For more than fifteen years Family Food has trusted us with its brands, and every new product is another chapter in a story we are proud to share.
